Age Calculator

Enter a date of birth to see the exact age today and the days left until the next birthday.

How age is calculated

The calculator counts full years from the date of birth, then the remaining full months, then the remaining days up to today. It also shows the total number of days lived and how many days remain until the next birthday.

Frequently asked questions

Does it handle leap years?

Yes. Total days are counted from real calendar dates, so leap days are included.

What if I was born on 29 February?

Your next birthday is counted from the calendar date in the following year, so in non-leap years it falls in March in this tool.

Can I use it for official documents?

Use it as a quick reference. For legal or official age requirements, follow the rules of the office or authority involved.

Results are estimates for general information. Check important figures with your bank, doctor or local authority.
