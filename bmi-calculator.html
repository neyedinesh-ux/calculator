How BMI is calculated

BMI = weight (kg) / height (m) squared. For example, 65 kg at 165 cm is 65 / (1.65 x 1.65), about 23.9.

BMICategory
Below 18.5Underweight
18.5 to 24.9Normal
25 to 29.9Overweight
30 and aboveObese

These are the general adult categories. Some guidelines for South and East Asian adults use lower cut-off points, so ask a doctor which applies to you.

Limits of BMI

BMI does not measure body fat. It can read high for muscular people and low for people who have lost muscle. It is not meant for children, teenagers or pregnant women.

Frequently asked questions

Is BMI a diagnosis?

No. It is a screening number. Talk to a doctor before making health decisions.

What is the normal weight range for my height?

The calculator shows the weight range that gives a BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 for the height you enter.
