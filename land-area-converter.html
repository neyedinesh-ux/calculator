Land Area Converter

Convert katha, decimal, bigha, acre, square feet and square metres in one step.

Assumes 1 katha = 720 sq ft, 1 bigha = 20 katha, 1 decimal = 435.6 sq ft. Katha and bigha sizes vary by region, so check the size stated in your land deed.

Check the local size

The katha and bigha are not the same everywhere. The values above are commonly used in Bangladesh, but the size differs in some regions and countries. Always confirm the unit size written in your land deed or with the local land office before buying, selling or measuring.
