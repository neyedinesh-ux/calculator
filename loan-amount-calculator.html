Loan Affordability Calculator

Start from the monthly payment you can afford and find the loan amount that fits it.

How it works

This is the EMI formula worked backwards. Given a monthly payment M, a monthly rate r and n payments:

Loan = M x (1 - (1 + r)^-n) / r

A longer term allows a bigger loan for the same monthly payment, but you pay more interest in total. The result also shows the total you repay and the interest portion.

Frequently asked questions

Will a lender approve this amount?

Not necessarily. Lenders also consider income, existing debts and credit history. Treat the result as a planning figure.

Should I borrow the maximum?

Many people keep a margin so that rising costs or lower income do not make payments hard to meet.
