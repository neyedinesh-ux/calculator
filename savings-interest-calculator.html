Savings Interest Calculator

See what a deposit grows to, with simple interest or compounding at different intervals.

Formulas

Compound interest: A = P x (1 + r/k)^(k x t), where P is the deposit, r the annual rate, k the number of compounding periods per year and t the time in years.

Simple interest: A = P x (1 + r x t).

The effective yearly rate shows what the stated rate is really worth after compounding.

Frequently asked questions

Is compound interest better than simple interest?

For the same rate and term, compounding gives a higher final amount because interest earns interest.

Does it include tax or bank charges?

No. Tax and fees depend on your country and account, so check them with your bank.

Does more frequent compounding matter?

Slightly. Monthly compounding gives a little more than yearly at the same rate.
